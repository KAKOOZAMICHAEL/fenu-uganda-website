How We Work

FENU works in many different ways to influence change and campaign for better education across Uganda. As a coalition, FENU believes civil society is strongest when it works together.

Government Lobbying

FENU engages directly with Uganda's Ministry of Education and Sports and line ministries, participating in policy reviews and acting as the civil society voice-channel.

National Campaigns

FENU runs targeted public campaigns on education issues to raise awareness, shift attitudes, and pressure decision-makers for systemic change.

Media Engagement

Working with the press to amplify education stories, FENU ensures that education issues remain visible in national public discourse.

Information Sharing

FENU connects its 100+ member organizations, sharing policy updates, research, best practices, and resources across the network.

Accountability

FENU holds those in power accountable - tracking government commitments on education and publishing findings to citizens and stakeholders.

Events & Conferences

From district-level education forums to national conferences and parliamentary workshops, FENU convenes key stakeholders for action.
