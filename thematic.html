Thematic Working Groups

FENU members come together in six thematic working groups to campaign on the most pressing education issues in Uganda. Each group drives focused advocacy, research, and policy engagement in its area.

Early Childhood Education

Advocates for accessible, quality early learning environments for all Ugandan children from birth to age 8, focusing on school readiness and foundational skills.

Adult Literacy

Promotes lifelong learning opportunities for adults, particularly in rural and underserved communities, ensuring literacy is recognized as a basic human right.

Gender & Girls' Education

Campaigns for 100% education completion rates for girls, tackling barriers at puberty, improving parental attitudes, and ending exploitation and abuse within the education system.

Access & Quality

Works to ensure every child in Uganda can access a school that delivers genuinely high-quality education, regardless of location, background, or ability.

Emergency in Education

Ensures education continues during crises - including displacement, conflict, and natural disasters - protecting the learning rights of the most vulnerable children.

Accountability in Education

Holds government, schools, and stakeholders accountable for education commitments, promoting transparency in resource allocation and policy delivery.
