About FENU Uganda

A national coalition advancing sustainable quality education for all.

Who We Are

A National Coalition for Education Justice

The Forum for Education NGOs in Uganda (FENU) is a national coalition of civil society organizations collectively standing up for the right to education for all children in Uganda. FENU coordinates NGOs, CBOs, and education institutions in joint advocacy, policy engagement, and public campaigns that strengthen education access, quality, equity, and accountability across the country.

Our History

Founded in March 2001

FENU was founded in March 2001 to unify education-focused civil society organizations under one collaborative platform to influence policy and improve education outcomes nationwide.

In its early years, the coalition's advocacy aligned with the Millennium Development Goals (MDGs), especially universal primary education, equity, and access for underserved communities.

As global and national priorities evolved, FENU deepened its work under the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), growing its coalition and strengthening engagement with national and local education stakeholders to drive system-level change.

Mission

To improve quality, access, equity and sustainability of education for all in Uganda

Vision

A Uganda where injustices in education policy and delivery have been overcome

Identity Statement

FENU aims to play a lead role in articulating the interests and expectations for a quality and equitable education for all

Our Values

Our Core Values

Mutual Respect

We believe in the inherent capacity and contribution of community institutions, international agencies, donors and our member organizations

Solidarity

Our commitment is grounded in standing up for the rights to a full and empowering Education for all.

Integrity

We act candidly and transparently in all the work we do and accept responsibility for our collective and individual actions

Excellence

We invariably challenge ourselves to the highest levels of learning and performance to achieve greater impact

Freedom from Control

We work independently of any religious, political or any other ideological affiliation or any other interests (overt or otherwise) so as to equitably serve the cause of education in Uganda

Coalition Work

Our Guiding Principles

Policy Influence

We work to influence national education policies and plans to ensure they are inclusive, equitable, and properly resourced.

Social Accountability

We empower local communities to monitor education delivery and hold providers and local governments accountable.

Media Engagement

We partner with media houses and journalists to spark informed public debate on critical education issues and policy gaps.

Evidence Generation

We conduct rigorous research to highlight what works in classrooms and guide our advocacy with solid evidence.

Equity

We champion the educational rights of the most marginalized, demanding special focus on girls, poor children, and remote areas.

Inclusion

We advocate for educational systems that welcome and accommodate children with special learning needs and physical disabilities.

Capacity Building

We strengthen the coordination and advocacy skills of local civil society networks to drive bottom-up accountability.

Lobbying Government

We maintain direct, constructive dialogue with the Ministry of Education to keep civil society insights at the decision table.
