About FENU

Uganda's Voice for Quality Education Since 2001

The Forum for Education NGOs in Uganda (FENU) is a national coalition of civil society organizations collectively standing up for the right to education for all children in Uganda. Founded in 2001, FENU coordinates over 100 NGOs, CBOs, and education institutions in joint advocacy, policy engagement, and campaigns for change.

Voluntary coalition of education civil society organizations

Direct working relationship with Uganda's Ministry of Education and Sports

Guided by the UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)
